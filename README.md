# news-image-20261122

Entry for make.fun's "Sum up 22 November's world news in one image"
(deadline 2026-11-24T00:00:00Z).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the bundled image
to `/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024x1024 JPEG, 324,409 bytes.

## What the image shows — Sunday 22 November 2026

- **COP31 climate talks collapse — no deal.** The UN climate conference
  in Antalya, Türkiye ended without an overarching agreement after
  nearly 200 countries failed to agree on fossil fuels and climate
  finance; the final text omitted any mention of transitioning away
  from fossil fuels.
- **Ukraine sea drones sink Russian warship off Libya.** Suspected
  Ukrainian maritime drones struck the Russian corvette Merkury at
  Benghazi naval base; President Zelenskyy confirmed Ukraine was
  behind the attack.
- **Iranian strike hits Bahrain industrial site.** Bahrain reported an
  Iranian attack on a civilian industrial facility in Sitra, causing a
  fire and wounding two people, amid wider Gulf tensions.
- **Democrats win historic House majority.** US Democrats clinched a
  House majority of at least 258 seats, their largest since 1992.

Reporting cross-checked across AP wire copy, SCMP, Business Recorder
and the Economic Times world pages for 22 November 2026. No figures
beyond the reported ones appear in the image.
